About EcoSort AI

EcoSort AI helps you recycle correctly. Upload a photo of your item and the app uses machine learning to sort it into a material category — paper, plastic, glass, metal, cardboard or general trash — then tells you exactly how to recycle it.

SmolVLM2 500M automatically looks at your photo through Ollama, identifies the material and explains its decision. If Ollama or the model is unavailable, the app displays an error instead of substituting a different classifier.
